**Does it make sense?** Yes. The records answer what §7's plan review asked: every round line carries provider, version, cost, latency, opportunity, purpose, and provenance; the report binds its commit by content identity; the export carries a version the checker asserts; every non-final finding keeps its transition. **Did it break anything else?** No. All five self-test suites stay green (507, 26, 26, 47, 29), `validate` is clean at 0 fatal, the ledger regenerates with the new findings, and the report/export are additive: `--check` output is unchanged, and no existing review file was touched.
