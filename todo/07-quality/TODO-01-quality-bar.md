## 3. Conformance Check and Its Report

> **Started:** 2026-11-09T02:47:16Z

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Validated 2026-11-09 before implementation. The evaluability rule, the tool inventory, and three stale facts corrected.**
>
> **A clause is gate-failing only when its owner has shipped.** The profile's 33 clauses name owners across six domains, most of them open (the framework, the repair contract, docs, release do not exist yet), so a check that fails the gate on every unmet clause would be permanently red and would teach everyone to ignore it. The rule this section builds: a clause whose owner section is `[x]` (the graph decides, `resolve` exit 3) evaluates to pass or fail and a fail fails the gate; a clause whose owner is open reports `not-yet-evaluable` with the owner named, in the matrix, never as a pass and never as a gate failure. The same split governs methods: `exists`, `absent`, `search`, and `count` evaluate now from sources and artifacts; `run`, `readback`, and `capture` clauses evaluate when their owner ships and extends the check, which each owner section now owes. `C24` is the one `run` clause evaluable today, from the build artifacts and `todo/.tidy-baseline`, because its owner `D00 T01 §3` shipped.
>
